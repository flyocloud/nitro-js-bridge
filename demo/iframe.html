Embedded Preview

Every element with a data-flyo-uid is registered with highlightAndClick(). Hover one: after a short delay the highlight ring and the pencil fade in, click the pencil to send openEdit to the editor panel. The whole page shares a single ring + button, so the cases below are really about whether the overlay lands on the right box — and disappears when it should.
